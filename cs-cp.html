Customer Service Configuration

Redesigning Pega Customer Service implementation for faster setups, clearer guidance, and stronger sales impact.

Case SummaryThe challengeUnderstanding the ProblemMy goal as UX Design LeadMy roleThe SolutionFinal DesignInteraction PrototypeOutcomes & ImpactReflection

Case Summary

Pega Customer Service is an omni-channel contact center solution built on the Pega Platform, enabling seamless, personalized experiences across voice, chat, email, and self-service channels.

The challenge

Customers who purchased the solution often struggled with a fragmented implementation process, scattered across Pega's Dev Studio. This required deep technical knowledge to locate and configure features individually, with no clear visibility into overall progress—leading to frustration and delayed time-to-value.

Understanding the Problem

Through user interviews, key pain points emerged:

  • Configuration steps felt overwhelming and disjointed for non-developers.
  • No centralized view of progress or status.
  • Missed opportunities to showcase core capabilities during sales demos.

Insight: Users needed a "control center" that provided clarity, progress tracking, and guidance—while highlighting Pega CS's value to drive adoption and sales.

My goal as UX Design Lead

Transform this into a guided, intuitive experience in App Studio (Pega's low-code authoring environment), empowering non-technical administrators to configure the application quickly while turning the UX into a competitive selling point.

My role

As UX Design Lead for Pega Customer Service, I owned the end-to-end design process. I collaborated closely with the project manager and development leads across multiple scrum teams to align on vision and iterate rapidly. This included:

  • Conducting user research (interviews with developers, administrators, and product owners)
  • Defining requirements and documenting solutions
  • Creating high-fidelity UI designs and interactive prototypes
  • Running usability testing sessions for validation

The Solution

We created a centralized Configuration Control Panel in App Studio:

  • Single landing page consolidating all CS features into intuitive categories and tiles.
  • Progress indicators (Not Started, In Progress, Complete) for at-a-glance status.
  • Drill-down views revealing detailed feature lists and configurations.
  • Multi-layered capability map to explore and enable core functionalities.
  • AI Buddy integration for contextual guidance pulled from Pega documentation and help sites.
  • Analytics integration to visualize application usage and demonstrate ROI.

Final Design

Refined tiles with status badges and deeper drill-downs for granular control—balancing simplicity with power.

Interaction Prototype

I built high-fidelity click-through prototypes to simulate the flow, incorporating existing design system patterns and proposing new ones. These were crucial for usability testing, developer handoff, and stakeholder buy-in.

Outcomes & Impact

Rolled out over two release cycles, the new control panel transformed the implementation experience, leading to:

  • Drastic improvement in user attitude—administrators reported feeling guided and confident, reducing setup frustration.
  • Faster time-to-value with clear progress tracking and rapid configuration.
  • Seamless integration with Pega Blueprint for even quicker application scaffolding.
  • enhancing the sales process, showcasing intuitive setup during demos, and driving customer interest.

Reflection

This project reinforced the power of user-centered consolidation in enterprise tools. By focusing on clarity and progress, we turned a technical necessity into a delightful experience—and a business advantage.
