Customer Advisory Board

This presentation, delivered to Pega's Customer Advisory Board (CAB), showcases upcoming enhancements in Pega Customer Service designed to elevate customer experience, boost agent productivity, and drive higher satisfaction scores. Through live demonstration of new concepts, the session illustrates close collaboration with customers to gather real-time feedback, foster ongoing dialogue, and validate feature innovations that address real-world service challenges.

Pega Call

Exploring Pega Call's integration with computer telephony middleware (such as Cisco), this demonstration explains Computer Telephony Integration (CTI) technology and its role in enabling seamless phone-computer coordination for call center agents. Highlighting the redesigned CTI user experience in Pega release 7.31, including the new screen sharing capability, the presentation demonstrates how these updates streamline agent-customer interactions, resolve issues more efficiently, and enhance overall call handling productivity.

My Pega

This early concept presentation introduces the vision for the My Pega portal as a personalized communication hub between Pega sales representatives and prospects. By delivering tailored content relevant to each prospect's needs and guiding them with targeted actions, the portal accelerates progress through the customer decision journey. The case study illustrates how this innovative approach strengthens sales engagement, builds stronger relationships, and drives more effective B2B conversions.
