Voice AI and smart decisioning

Real-time guidance and form auto-fill for agents on live calls.

Project OverviewRole & ResponsibilitiesChallengeSolution & Key FeaturesOutcomes & ImpactReflections & Learnings

Project Overview

This project focused on leveraging Pega Voice AI and smart decisioning to transform customer service interactions. Pega Voice AI acts as an intelligent co-pilot for customer service representatives (CSRs) and sales agents during live phone calls. It uses real-time speech-to-text transcription, natural language processing (NLP), and emotion detection to analyze conversations and provide contextual suggestions.

Integrated with Pega's Customer Decision Hub (the "decisioning brain"), the solution shifts agents from a purely reactive mode—handling issues as they arise—to a proactive one. It enables intelligent guidance for building customer loyalty, reducing churn, and identifying revenue opportunities through next-best-actions. Key features include form auto-fill, real-time action suggestions, and post-call analytics.

The envisioned UX emphasizes seamless, hands-free support to reduce agent effort, minimize screen switching, and enhance overall productivity.

Role & Responsibilities

As a UX designer, responsibilities included:

  • Designing an enhanced user experience for the agent interface in Pega Customer Service.
  • Creating adaptive models and UX flows that integrate Voice AI insights with smart decisioning.

Documenting the experience through a design walkthrough, including a video demonstration of the Form Auto-fill feature (see sample below).

Challenge

Traditional customer service interactions place agents in a reactive posture: they respond to customer issues but often miss opportunities to deepen relationships or upsell. Agents frequently:

  • Manually search for information.
  • Enter data repetitively.
  • Rely on colleagues for guidance.
  • This leads to longer call times, higher agent frustration, inconsistent service, and lost revenue potential.

The goal was to empower agents with real-time, context-aware intelligence without overwhelming their workflow.

Solution & Key Features

  • Real-Time Intelligent Guidance → As the conversation unfolds, Voice AI detects intent (e.g., "adding a newborn to insurance") and suggests relevant case types or workflows.
  • Form Auto-Fill → Extracts entities from speech (names, addresses, dates) and pre-populates forms, enabling hands-free data entry.
  • Smart Decisioning Integration → Uses Pega's Next-Best-Action framework to recommend proactive offers or retention actions, balancing customer needs with business goals (e.g., loyalty-building bundles).
  • Post-Call Analytics → Generates summaries and insights to refine future recommendations.
  • Emotion & Script Adherence → (Supported by Pega) Detects sentiment and guides script compliance for consistent, empathetic interactions.

The UX prioritizes unobtrusive notifications, clear visual hierarchy in the guidance panel, and one-click acceptance of suggestions to keep agents focused on the customer.

Outcomes & Impact

While specific metrics were not detailed in the project documentation, Pega's Voice AI and Decision Hub typically deliver:

  • Reduced average handle time through automation and fewer clicks.
  • Improved agent productivity and reduced training ramp-up.
  • Higher customer satisfaction from faster, more personalized resolutions.
  • Increased revenue via proactive next-best-actions and reduced churn.
  • The design walkthrough effectively communicated how the enhanced UX makes every agent perform like a top performer.

Reflections & Learnings

This project highlights the power of embedding AI directly into operational workflows rather than as a separate tool. By combining Voice AI's conversational intelligence with smart decisioning, the solution creates a truly proactive service model. Later iterations explored deeper emotion-based routing and multi-channel consistency (e.g., extending to messaging AI).

Overall, the project demonstrates how thoughtful UX design in enterprise platforms like Pega can turn complex AI capabilities into intuitive, empowering experiences for frontline agents.
