Omar BenmoussaUX Design Specialist

For more than 15 years, I've designed user experiences for complex enterprise products—the kind of systems where orders, accounts, cases, and workflows all have to make sense to very different people. At Pegasystems, I led UI design for CRM products used in banking, telecom, utilities, healthcare, and beyond, and extended the Cosmos design system with new patterns and components so the experience stayed consistent as the product line grew.

Working across so many industries taught me that most organizations face the same core challenge: serving a wide range of users without the product fragmenting along the way. I bring that perspective to every project—grounding decisions in research, prototyping early, and working closely with product owners, developers, and engineering teams so that designs are feasible, aligned with business goals, and make complex work feel simple.

Outside of work, I recharge through road and mountain biking, running, home DIY projects, and spending time with my family—sharing knowledge and adventures with my kids.
