Enhancing B2B Interactions in Pega Customer Service

Transition to Constellation Architecture

Project OverviewObjectivesSolutionOutcomes

Project Overview

Pega Customer Service is a comprehensive CRM solution designed to handle both B2C (business-to-consumer) and B2B (business-to-business) interactions. In B2B scenarios, customer service representatives (CSRs) often deal with complex hierarchical relationships, including business accounts, service accounts, and multiple associated contacts. A key challenge arose during the platform's evolution from the legacy UI-kit architecture to the modern Constellation architecture and Cosmos design system: recreating the "calling on behalf" interaction type without introducing regressions for existing B2B and B2B2C implementations.

This feature allows CSRs to initiate interactions on behalf of a specific contact within a business account, ensuring accurate context, authorization checks, and seamless service case management across organizational hierarchies.

Objectives

  • Preserve critical B2B functionalities, such as account-based searching, hierarchical customer data views, and distinct interaction handling in the Interaction Portal.
  • Maintain differentiation between B2B (multi-contact, account-centric) and B2C (individual-centric) workflows.
  • Enable smooth upgrades for customers with established B2B setups.
  • Align the user experience with Pega's updated design system while supporting advanced B2B relationship management.

Solution

The team successfully recreated the "calling on behalf" interaction type in the new architecture. This involved:

  • Implementing robust search mechanisms using account identifiers.
  • Supporting hierarchical views of customer data (accounts → service accounts → contacts).
  • Ensuring the new UX added to the Interaction Portal clearly distinguishes B2B interactions, providing CSRs with contextual tools for managing multi-party business relationships.

Outcomes

Customers with B2B and hybrid B2B2C configurations could upgrade to the latest Pega Customer Service version seamlessly, retaining full functionality. The enhanced B2B support improved CSR efficiency in handling complex enterprise inquiries, resulting in better service delivery and increased customer satisfaction in business-oriented environments.
