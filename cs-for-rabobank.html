CS for Rabobank

A Rabobank-branded vision prototype built on Pega's Cosmos design system.

Case OverviewThe challengeMy goalMy RoleProcess & SolutionOutcomes & ImpactReflection

Case Overview

Rabobank, a long-standing Pega Customer Service customer, was running on an older architecture. With the launch of Pega's new Cosmos design system, there was an opportunity to re-engage the client and demonstrate the value of upgrading.

The challenge

Customers like Rabobank often hesitate to upgrade due to uncertainty about the new look, feel, and capabilities. Abstract presentations fall flat—stakeholders need to see the future in their own context.

My goal

To create a compelling, Rabobank-branded vision prototype using real client data and workflows to spark excitement, gather feedback, and drive upgrade commitments.

My Role

As UX Designer, I led the visioning exercise in close collaboration with:

  • The account executive and members of the consulting team
  • Key Rabobank stakeholders

I translated their real-world workflows and data into high-fidelity screens, ensuring full alignment with the new Cosmos design system while incorporating Rabobank's branding.

Process & Solution

Leveraging actual Rabobank data points, interactions, and workflows, I designed a series of key screens showcasing core Customer Service interaction points:

  • Customer 360° views with personalized insights
  • Case management and omni-channel interactions
  • Agent workspaces with modern UI patterns
  • Self-service portals and guided journeys

All designs adhered to the new Cosmos design system for consistency, performance, and future-proofing—while applying Rabobank's visual identity (colors, logos, typography).

Outcomes & Impact

The prototype transformed conversations with Rabobank:

  • Sparked excitement about the modern, intuitive experience the upgrade would deliver.
  • Drove meaningful feedback on features and priorities, enriching our roadmap.
  • Strengthened client commitment, reinforcing Pega's partnership and opening doors to upgrade discussions.
  • Served as a powerful sales tool, demonstrating immediate value and differentiating Pega in a competitive market.

Reflection

Vision prototypes like this bridge the gap between "possible" and "real" for enterprise clients. By grounding abstract upgrades in the client's own world, we not only build trust but turn design into a strategic driver for retention and growth.
